BEFORE WE GET MOVING.

What should I include in a freight enquiry?

Include collection and delivery locations, the goods description, package count, dimensions, weight and when the shipment will be ready. Mention any handling or timing requirements.

Is overnight delivery available for every route?

Service availability, collection cut-offs and delivery expectations must be confirmed for your particular origin and destination. The website does not guarantee a transit time.

Can I enquire about customs clearing?

Yes. Choose in-house customs clearing in the enquiry form and describe the goods, the movement and the assistance you need. The required documentation can then be discussed.

Does this demonstration send my enquiry?

This presentation builds a brief on your device. You can review, copy or download it. Nothing is sent automatically and this demo has no connected booking or quote system.
